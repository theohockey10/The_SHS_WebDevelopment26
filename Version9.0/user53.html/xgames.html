X Games & Pro Snowboarding

The worlds best snowboarders competing at the highest level

What is the X Games?

The X Games is one of the biggest extreme sports competitions in the world, featuring the best snowboarders on the planet. Athletes compete in events like Big Air, Rail Jam, and Halfpipe where style, difficulty, and control decide the winner.

Big Air

Riders launch off massive jumps and perform one major trick in the air. Scoring is based on height, rotation, difficulty, and clean landings.

🔥 Notable Moment

Big Air has produced some of the most extreme snowboard tricks ever seen, including massive spins and near-perfect landings under pressure.

Rail Jam

Rail Jam events focus on creativity and control as riders perform tricks on rails, boxes, and urban-style features. Style, flow, and technical skill are key.

🔥 Notable Moment

Some of the most impressive runs include technical rail combinations, switch tricks, and creative freestyle lines that push progression forward.

Halfpipe

In Halfpipe, riders move back and forth in a U-shaped course performing multiple aerial tricks. Judges look for height, speed, difficulty, and smooth execution.

🔥 Notable Moment

Perfect 100-point runs have been achieved in the Halfpipe, showcasing flawless control, massive air, and elite-level trick difficulty.
